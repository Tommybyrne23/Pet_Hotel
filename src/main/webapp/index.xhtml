Whether you're away for a day, a weekend, or an extended holiday,
our dedicated team is committed to providing a safe, comfortable, and caring environment for every pet in our care.

Services

Dog Boarding

Our spacious dog boarding facilities provide a safe and comfortable environment for dogs of all breeds and sizes.
Each guest receives daily exercise, regular feeding, fresh water, and plenty of attention from our experienced staff.
We aim to make every stay enjoyable, whether ist's for a weekend or an extended holiday.

Cat Boarding

Our cat accommodation offers a quiet and relaxing environment designed to reduce stress while you're away.
Each cat has its own clean, comfortable space for daily feeding, fresh litter, and regular care from our team.
We strive to ensure every feline guest feels secure and well looked after.

Reptile Boarding

Our reptile boarding service is designed to meet the specialised needs of reptiles and other exotic pets.
Enclosures are maintained with appropriate heating, lighting, and humidity where required.
Our trained staff ensure feeding schedules and habitat conditions are carefully followed throughout your pet's stay.

Bird Boarding

We provide spacious, hygienic cages suitable for a variety of bird species.
Birds are cared for with daily feeding, fresh water, cage cleaning, and regular interaction where appropriate.
Our staff work to maintain a calm environment that supports your bird's health and wellbeing.
